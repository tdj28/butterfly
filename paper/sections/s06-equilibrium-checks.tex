\section{Both equilibria and their local spectral conditions}
\label{supp:equilibria}

The equilibrium equation is $az^2-cz+b=0$, with
$q_\pm=(az_\pm,-z_\pm,z_\pm)$. For the positive parameters considered here,
we avoid cancellation in the small root by evaluating
\[
 z_-=\frac{2b}{c+\sqrt{c^2-4ab}},\qquad
 z_+=\frac{c+\sqrt{c^2-4ab}}{2a}.
\]
At an equilibrium, setting $r=c-az$ gives the characteristic polynomial
\[
 \lambda^3+(r-a)\lambda^2+(1+z-ar)\lambda+(r-az).
\]
These are algebraic checks at declared parameter values, not a new
homoclinic or manifold computation.

The public script \texttt{scripts/report\_review001\_equilibria.py}
evaluates both roots with the existing model code and independently with
80-digit Decimal arithmetic. A bounded bisection of the cubic's real root
and the remaining quadratic factor are compared with the NumPy Jacobian
eigensolver. A second comparison uses precisely the same binary64 Jacobian
entries, separating eigensolver differences from parameter and equilibrium
rounding. The source-bound machine-readable report is
\texttt{paper/tables/review001-equilibria.json}.

In forward time, $q_-$ has one stable and two unstable directions at every
listed point; $q_+$ has two stable and one unstable direction. The sum of
the real eigenvalue and the real part of one complex eigenvalue changes
sign under time reversal. For $q_-$ the standard one-real-unstable
Shilnikov convention therefore uses reversed time: its saddle quantity is
$10.21407717$, $10.21260015$, $19.92417484$, and $19.92414297$, respectively.
For $q_+$ the same convention is already forward-time, giving positive
quantities $0.17648446$, $0.17925086$, $0.14689616$, and $0.14695788$.
This explicitly records the spectral convention; it does not infer the
existence of a loop from a sign.

In the archived check, the largest binary64 equilibrium residual is
$7.42\times10^{-14}$ and the largest same-Jacobian spectral discrepancy
is $6.44\times10^{-15}$. Decimal polynomial-component residuals are below
$2.19\times10^{-69}$ across both comparisons. Working precision is not a certified accuracy bound:
no interval arithmetic or validated manifold enclosure is used. Neither
equilibrium's role in global reinjection has been established or excluded
by these local calculations.
