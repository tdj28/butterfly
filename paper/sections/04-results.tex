\section{Established computational results}

\subsection{Reference equilibrium at the proposed hub}

At $(a,b,c)=(0.1798,0.2,10.3084)$, the equilibrium equations have a small
solution
\[
(x_*,y_*,z_*)=(0.003489598512,-0.019408223091,0.019408223091).
\]
The analytic Jacobian eigenvalues are
$0.0889667722\pm0.9959555077i$ and $-10.3030439458$.  Thus this equilibrium is
a saddle focus with a two-dimensional unstable spiral eigenspace and one
strongly stable real direction.  Central-difference Jacobian checks,
vector-field residuals, and equilibrium invariance under DOP853 pass.  This
verifies the local eigenstructure asserted at the proposed hub coordinate; it
does not exhibit a homoclinic orbit or prove uniqueness on a parameter segment.

\subsection{Analytic small-equilibrium Hopf locus}

Writing an equilibrium as $(az,-z,z)$ and defining $r=b/z=c-az$, the
characteristic polynomial is $\lambda^3+A\lambda^2+B\lambda+C$, with
$A=r-a$, $B=1+z-ar$, and $C=r-az$.  The Hopf condition $AB=C$ therefore
reduces exactly to
\[
a(1+r^2-ar)=b,
\]
after which $z=b/r$ and $c=az+r$.  At fixed $b=0.2$, all 192 sampled points
over $a\in[0.1,0.195]$, including the exact hub abscissa, pass independent
eigensystem root and transverse-sign checks.  The locus covers
$c\in[0.4218296645,1.0702742041]$.  At $a=0.1798$ its value is
$c=0.5192306256940273$, giving a fixed-$a$ separation of
$9.789169374305974$ from the reported hub coordinate.  This qualifies the
local Hopf endpoint of an L2-like slice; it neither continues the period-1
cycle to the hub nor establishes the proposed homoclinic endpoint.

A pilot-informed, unchanged-gate successor then phase-corrects the same
one-winding period-1 family at 118 points from $c=c_H+0.001$ to the exact hub.
Every point passes closure, phase, neutral-multiplier, trajectory, and winding
gates, and six checkpoints pass independent Radau correction.  The near-Hopf
RMS amplitude exponent is $0.5017311$ ($R^2=0.9999983$), while the nearest
period differs from the linear Hopf value by $0.001417$.  A real $-1$ Floquet
crossing is initially bracketed in $c\in[3.1556294737,3.2536126316]$.
A coupled four-node orbit--parameter--anti-periodic-tangent solve then locates
the event at
\[
c_{1\to2}=3.1807265333384103.
\]
All coupled residuals are below $4.76\times10^{-15}$; four cyclic monodromy
products have median multiplier $-1.0000000000000067$ and spread
$1.56\times10^{-15}$.  Independent Radau monodromy gives
$-0.9999999999998008$, closure $3.50\times10^{-14}$, and winding one.  This
qualifies the parent flip.  A doubled-cover shooting nullspace switch then
continues both transverse signs for 24 points on the post-flip side.  At the
frozen common checkpoint $c=3.1845$, independent Radau correction gives the
parent multiplier $-1.0021368076$ and child multiplier $0.9914596621$.  The
two signs agree up to phase with whole-orbit RMS $7.82\times10^{-8}$ and phase
shift $0.49739$.  Their period ratio is $1.9999363$, half-period closure fails
by approximately $0.193$, and each child winds twice.  Perturbed forward
integration recovers the child with phase-invariant RMS
$3.38\times10^{-10}$.  This establishes a primitive stable period-2 child and
the first supercritical rung on this explicit path.

Pseudo-arclength continuation then preserves that primitive child through 83
points and brackets its first real $-1$ crossing.  A coupled eight-node solve
locates the exact second event at
\[
c_{2\to4}=4.3100451384813105,
\]
with period $11.724290361610073$.  The reference flip residual is
$3.94\times10^{-14}$; independent Radau gives $5.91\times10^{-13}$, closure
$1.12\times10^{-12}$, half-period nonclosure $2.8615$, and winding two.  A
second doubled-cover nullspace switch opens both local child arms.  At the
frozen checkpoint $c=4.318$, the arms agree up to phase with whole-orbit RMS
$3.14\times10^{-7}$ and shift $0.50140$.  Independent Radau gives the unstable
period-2 parent multiplier $-1.0115948996$ and stable period-4 child multiplier
$0.9535193007$.  The period ratio is $2.000002$, the child winds four times,
and perturbed integration recovers it to RMS $2.83\times10^{-9}$.  This
establishes the second complete supercritical rung.  Periods beyond four and
symbolic ordering remain separate gates.

At the reported hub the continued orbit has period $5.9935437090$, primary
nontrivial multiplier $-3.2022459546$, and minimum distance
$10.0310033361$ from the small equilibrium.  It is therefore a finite unstable
period-1 orbit, not the proposed homoclinic orbit.  This does not reject a
distinct stable/unstable equilibrium-manifold connection; it prevents the two
objects from being conflated.

\subsection{Flow bifurcation versus section geometry}

After the period-5 to period-10 flip, the stable period-10 child changes its
crossing count on the historical return section near
$b=0.181750232321$.  Continuous tangency refinement identifies this as a
section-boundary grazing.  It is therefore not another bifurcation of the flow
orbit.  This result is directly relevant to branch-count studies: an apparent
change in a scalar return plot can originate in the measurement construction,
so a topology-transition classifier must include section-robustness tests.

\subsection{Long transient capture}

Cases that initially appeared to show coexistence of a chaotic attractor and a
stable periodic attractor were followed for longer horizons and with multiple
initial conditions.  The trajectories eventually entered the stable periodic
windows.  Persistent multistability is rejected for those tested cases; the
evidence instead supports long chaotic-transient capture, consistent with a
nonattracting chaotic-saddle mechanism.  Computing the saddle itself remains a
separate acceptance test.
