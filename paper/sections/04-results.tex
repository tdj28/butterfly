\section{Established computational results}

\subsection{Identity-safe local cascade}

At fixed $(a,c)=(0.245,5.1)$, the true period-5 family undergoes eight resolved
supercritical flips.  The first six parent-event values are
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
b_1&=0.1834675907716,\quad b_2=0.1805372082024,\\
b_3&=0.1798912237616,\quad b_4=0.1797506213663,\\
b_5&=0.1797203688505,\quad b_6=0.1797138833005,
\end{split}
\end{equation}
and the resolved period-320 to period-640 event is
$b_7=0.17971249399393$.  The associated finite spacing ratios are
$4.5363$, $4.5944$, $4.6476$, $4.6646$, and $4.6682$.  Multiple shooting,
block-Floquet stability, and whole-orbit identity establish a stable period-640
child.  These are finite numerical observations; proximity of the ratios to a
classical constant is not presented as a universality proof.

The period-640 parent has a corrected eighth flip event at
$b_8=0.1797121964322390$.  The resulting next finite spacing ratio is
$4.66899$.  At common parameter $b=0.17971215$, the two independently switched
signs agree up to phase with whole-orbit RMS $3.94\times10^{-8}$ and have
dominant nontrivial moduli $0.4261745532$ and $0.4261741560$.  This establishes
a stable period-1280 child and closes the eighth supercritical rung.

Three event locations were predicted from earlier rungs before their dedicated
search.  The frozen period-80 to period-160 prediction missed the resolved
event by $1.398\times10^{-7}$.  The frozen period-320 to period-640 prediction
missed by $3.00\times10^{-10}$.  The period-640 to period-1280 prediction at
$b=0.1797121964470$ differs from the corrected eighth event by only
$1.476\times10^{-11}$.  All three frozen prospective event-location tests
therefore succeed; period-1280 child qualification remains separate.

Subsequent baseline and tighter-solver scalar refinements retain a signed
interval only $3.22\times10^{-15}$ wide, but the closest precision-audited
point has multiplier residual $1.70\times10^{-8}$ and therefore misses the
preregistered $10^{-8}$ equality gate.  The block-cyclic multiplier and direct
monodromy products agree near $10^{-13}$, so the interval is retained as strong
event evidence but is not promoted to a corrected eighth event.  A coupled
orbit--parameter--eigenvector solve with an exact second-variational Jacobian
recovers the already resolved seventh event within $8.94\times10^{-13}$, with
orbit and tangent residuals $9.21\times10^{-13}$ and $1.01\times10^{-11}$.
Applied prospectively to the period-640 parent, the same formulation converges
in three evaluations with orbit and tangent residuals $1.46\times10^{-12}$ and
$7.81\times10^{-12}$; four direct monodromy products give
$-0.999999999810$.  Thus the scalar failures diagnose a numerical formulation
limit rather than absence of the crossing.

\subsection{Flow bifurcation versus section geometry}

After the period-5 to period-10 flip, the stable period-10 child changes its
crossing count on the historical return section near
$b=0.181750232321$.  Continuous tangency refinement identifies this as a
section-boundary grazing.  It is therefore not another bifurcation of the flow
orbit.  This result is directly relevant to branch-count studies: an apparent
change in a scalar return plot can originate in the measurement construction,
so a topology-transition classifier must include section-robustness tests.

\subsection{Long transient capture}

Cases that initially appeared to show coexistence of a chaotic attractor and a
stable periodic attractor were followed for longer horizons and with multiple
initial conditions.  The trajectories eventually entered the stable periodic
windows.  Persistent multistability is rejected for those tested cases; the
evidence instead supports long chaotic-transient capture, consistent with a
nonattracting chaotic-saddle mechanism.  Computing the saddle itself remains a
separate acceptance test.

\subsection{Qualified local three-branch return relation}

At the published chaotic control $(a,b,c)=(0.2,0.2,20)$, the historical
negative-oriented section unexpectedly gives a three-branch rather than the
prospectively expected two-branch scalar return relation.  A subsequently
frozen sensitivity matrix varies five nearby $(a,c)$ points, three section
offsets, seven binning/smoothing/prominence choices, and both nondegenerate
section coordinates.  All 105 negative-oriented $x$ cells and all 105
negative-oriented $z$ cells resolve as three branches.  Bootstrap consensus is
one in every cell, invariant-domain coverage is at least $0.94$, and the
conditional-spread ratio is at most $0.02502$.

The same scalar reduction does not survive indiscriminately on the opposite
oriented half-plane.  The positive-oriented $x$ relation is
bootstrap-unstable in 102 of 105 cells; the remaining three resolve as five
branches.  All 105 positive-oriented $z$ cells fail the preregistered
invariant-domain-coverage gate.  Thus the three-branch result is qualified as a
robust local property of the historically relevant oriented half-plane, not as
an orientation-independent scalar topological invariant.

The Barrio--Blesa--Serrano definition uses a distinct section through the small
equilibrium's $x$ coordinate with $\dot{x}>0$.  On this published section, a
separately frozen 84-cell matrix directly reproduces both Figure 2 controls.
At fixed $(b,c)=(0.2,20)$, all 42 $y$ and $z$ cells at $a=0.11$ resolve as a
unimodal two-branch map, while all 42 cells at $a=0.2$ resolve as a bimodal
three-branch map.  Every bootstrap consensus is one, coverage is at least
$0.98$, and conditional spread is at most $0.02389$.  This qualifies the two
attracting endpoints, not the intervening TBA curve or its continuation through
chaotic saddles in regular windows.

A prospectively frozen 21-point attractor scan on the intervening $a$ path
orders five resolved two-branch samples below eight resolved three-branch
samples and brackets an attracting-set transition band
$a\in[0.155,0.160]$.  Five points converge to period-4 attractors and three
more fail invariant-domain coverage; these are retained as gaps.  The lower
endpoint is uncertainty-marginal: $y$ returns two branches at exactly the
bootstrap threshold $0.80$, while $z$ is unresolved at $0.70$ and nominally
has two critical points.  At $a=0.160$, both coordinates return three with
consensus one.  This band is not identified with the invariant-saddle TBA,
which the published regular control already places on the bimodal side at
$a=0.149$.

The first prospectively frozen sprinkler reconstruction inside those two
period-4 windows does not pass its complete qualification gate.  At $a=0.118$,
2775 final survivors provide 23022 within-trajectory pairs per coordinate and
both coordinates resolve the published two branches with unit bootstrap
consensus.  At $a=0.149$, 1564 survivors provide 12892 pairs, but the frozen
three-percent global-prominence criterion suppresses the shallow left-hand
maximum and reports two branches.  A post-result diagnostic at lower
prominence exposes two critical points in both coordinates with unit bootstrap
consensus, matching the qualitative shape in the published saddle panel.  We
retain the prospective failure: local uncertainty, rather than a retrospectively
changed global cutoff, must qualify the shallow branch.  Pointwise fixed-step
versus DOP853 finite-time capture labels agree on only 25 of 32 audited seeds,
so long-horizon convergence will be tested at the ensemble-distribution level.

A second prospectively frozen experiment performs that test across baseline,
half-step, later-conditioning, shifted-grid, and coarser shifted-grid
ensembles.  All 300 case--run--coordinate--oracle cells recover two branches at
$a=0.118$ and three at $a=0.149$, with unit variant consensus and 15000 total
bootstrap refits.  Across-run normalized critical-location drift is at most
$0.01666$.  This qualifies the topology component over the declared
perturbations.  The complete experiment still fails: regular-grid survivor
fractions differ by as much as $0.07962$, exceeding the frozen $0.05$ gate,
and one linearly interpolated crossing time misses its short-horizon tolerance.
Scrambled low-discrepancy ensembles and higher-order event interpolation are
therefore required before qualifying the complete saddle sampler.

The next prospectively frozen experiment makes exactly those two changes
without relaxing a threshold.  Three independent scrambled Sobol ensembles,
nested sizes from 4096 through 16384, step halving, and later conditioning give
14 complete saddle runs.  All 420 topology cells recover two branches at
$a=0.118$ and three at $a=0.149$, with unit variant consensus and 21000
bootstrap refits.  The maximum survivor-fraction differences fall to $0.01013$
and $0.01135$, below the $0.05$ gate; maximum normalized critical drift is
$0.01485$.  Cubic-Hermite section events agree with DOP853 within
$2.46\times10^{-6}$ in scaled state and $3.16\times10^{-6}$ in time.  All
support and numerical-failure gates pass.  This qualifies the finite-time CPU
sprinkler reconstruction at both published regular-window controls, but not a
continued TBA curve or a topological-template equivalence.

A separately frozen Float64 GPU transfer reproduces these two controls on an
NVIDIA RTX A5000.  All 30 case--coordinate--oracle cells retain the expected
two/three distinction with unit variant consensus.  The six checkpoint
survivor counts are identical at $a=0.149$; at $a=0.118$ only the final count
differs, by one trajectory among 8192.  The largest combined CPU/GPU normalized
critical-location span is $0.01463$.  Ten short-horizon GPU trajectories agree
with DOP853 within $2.46\times10^{-6}$ in scaled state and
$3.16\times10^{-6}$ in event time.  This passes statistical backend parity at
the two controls; it is not an independent saddle construction or a continued
TBA.

The first independent-method experiment then uses strict PIM triples and an
adaptive DOP853 return map.  Its complete two-control gate fails: all five
unimodal access lines and two bimodal lines encounter a candidate that remains
uncaptured through the frozen 256-return lifetime ceiling.  The other three
bimodal lines complete 1200-return straddles.  Their 2997 retained pairs recover
three branches in both coordinates with all 15 oracle variants agreeing; the
largest combined CPU/PIM critical-location span is $0.01263$.  This is retained
as independent bimodal-saddle corroboration, not as a successful reproduction
of both controls.  The failure diagnoses zero censoring as incompatible with
dynamic restraint: a surviving candidate supplies a lifetime lower bound and
must be handled as right-censored data rather than discarded.

The prospectively frozen successor implements that lower-bound ordering on
three section lines and repeats the complete construction with censor ceilings
of 64 and 128 returns.  The full nested-horizon experiment fails.  At 64
returns, all three unimodal straddles complete but every oracle variant rejects
their projections for insufficient invariant-domain coverage; only one of
three bimodal lines completes, below the frozen straddle and pair-count gates.
No integration fails and no branch label is forced.

Both 128-return profiles independently pass.  Three unimodal straddles and
three bimodal straddles each provide 2097 pooled return pairs per coordinate.
Both coordinates recover respectively two and three branches in all 15 oracle
variants.  The largest within-PIM normalized critical-location span is
$0.01501$ and the largest combined sprinkler/PIM span is $0.01511$.  This is a
qualified fixed-horizon two-control corroboration by an adaptive-DOP853 orbit-
restraint method, but the failed 64-return profiles preclude a claim of censor-
horizon invariance.

A final frozen extension carries forward the exact 128-return intervals and
artifact hashes, then computes new 256-return straddles.  All six access lines
complete without integration failure.  Both coordinates again recover two
branches at $a=0.118$ and three at $a=0.149$ in all 15 oracle variants.  The
largest within-256, sprinkler/256, and frozen-128/new-256 normalized spans are
$0.01601$, $0.01595$, and $0.01601$.  Censored evaluations fall from 1108/385
at 128 returns to 34/1 at 256 returns, qualifying two-control finite-horizon
PIM stability over that range.

The first blind extension beyond those controls retains its failed complete
gate.  At $a=0.140$, all seven sprinkler ensembles, both coordinates, and all
210 oracle variants recover two branches, narrowing the resolved saddle
bracket to $[0.140,0.149]$.  At $a=0.145$, rapid survivor loss leaves only
284--1872 pairs: 72 of 90 attempted variants return two branches, none return
three, and 18 remain unresolved, so no label is assigned.  The $a=0.120$
oracle is unanimously two-branch but misses the frozen cycle-recurrence gate.
The resulting sequence must not yet be joined to the adjacent two-branch
$a=0.150$ aperiodic candidate from the shorter attractor scan without an
invariant-set identity and long-horizon audit.

That audit now shows persistent chaos but fails strict cross-resolution branch
consensus.  Across four burn-ins and four independent section seeds, both
Lyapunov cases classify chaotic and independent largest-exponent estimates
agree within $0.00865$.  Of 48 coarse 20-bin oracle cells, 46 return two; of
192 cells with 30--80 bins, 189 return three, with no opposite votes.  Thus the
old $a=0.150$ two label is not robust: adequate resolution repeatedly exposes
the shallow additional extremum.  The result remains failed pending a
prospective resolution model and a tighter trace-identity audit.

That successor passes on five new 2400-return datasets.  All 30 coarse 20-bin
control cells return two, while all 120 30--80-bin cells return three.  Maximum
combined critical drift against the frozen predecessor intervals is $0.01807$.
Both tighter-tolerance Lyapunov cases classify chaotic, agree with independent
largest-exponent estimates within $0.00519$, and reduce trace error to
$1.25\times10^{-7}$.  Thus $a=0.150$ is qualified as a chaotic three-branch set
at the declared adequate resolutions.

An eightfold-support successor then revisits the unresolved $a=0.145$ saddle.
All seven ensembles now exceed 323 final survivors and 2726 return pairs, with
no integration or event-audit failure.  All 105 $y$-coordinate oracle cells
resolve as two branches.  In $z$, 84 of 105 cells also resolve as two; the
remaining 21 are exactly the three 80-bin variants in every ensemble.  Each of
those cells fails only the frozen equal-width domain-coverage gate, at 0.675 or
0.6875 versus the required 0.7, while retaining one nominal critical point in
the interval recovered by the resolved cells.  No cell returns three branches.
The full two-coordinate gate therefore remains failed, but sample scarcity is
closed: the remaining decision concerns prospective treatment of bins outside
the projected invariant support.

The preregistered new-data successor passes that decision without revising the
oracle.  All 420 variants at the published controls resolve normally,
preserving two branches at $a=0.118$ and three at $a=0.149$.  At $a=0.145$,
all 105 $y$ variants and 84 $z$ variants resolve normally as two; the remaining
21 $z$ variants are exactly the declared coverage-only censors, with no
rejection or contrary count.  The weakest target run retains 327 survivors and
2768 return pairs, and maximum target across-run critical drift is $0.01495$.
All survival and DOP853/Hermite gates pass.  The ordered saddle labels are
therefore $2,2,3$, prospectively narrowing the sampled transition bracket to
$[0.145,0.149]$.

The first adaptive midpoint is then evaluated without an expected label.
Candidate counts two and three are tested separately under the qualified rule.
All 14 run--coordinate decisions at $a=0.147$ uniquely select two.  Of 210
variant cells, 207 resolve normally and three are admissible coverage censors;
none is rejected or returns three.  The weakest run retains 350 survivors and
3008 pairs, maximum across-run critical drift is $0.01499$, and every numerical
gate passes.  The sampled bracket consequently halves to $[0.147,0.149]$.

The next blind midpoint at $a=0.148$ fails cross-conditioning consensus while
passing its support and numerical gates.  All six 300-unit runs return three
branches in every $y$ variant and every 30--80-bin $z$ variant; their 20-bin
$z$ cells under-resolve or fail bootstrap stability.  The survivor subset

variants.  The weakest run still retains 200 survivors and 1737 pairs, survivor
fractions agree within $0.00403$, and no integration or event audit fails.  No
label is assigned and the bracket remains $[0.147,0.149]$.

The frozen nested-horizon successor also fails its all-run consensus gate, but
localizes the failure.  Across horizons 360--480, 12 of 16 blind
run--coordinate decisions select two branches and four are unassigned; none

exactly the 80-bin variants in the four unassigned decisions and fail

coordinates.  At 480 only 121 survivors and 1061 pairs remain; 20--60-bin

period, survival, and numerical gates pass.  Thus no resolved third branch
reappears at the longer horizons, but the strict finite-horizon label and
$[0.147,0.149]$ bracket remain unchanged.

An independent blind PIM successor removes final-survivor attrition from the
decision and passes.  All three fixed access lines complete at both 128- and
256-return right-censor ceilings.  Each profile supplies 2097 post-burn-in
pairs per coordinate.  Both $y$ and $z$ select two branches in every one of 15
oracle variants at both horizons: 60 of 60 cells are two-branch, with none

Thus the finite-horizon PIM saddle at $a=0.148$ is independently qualified as
two-branch, and the sampled classifier bracket narrows to $[0.148,0.149]$.

The identical blind PIM definition is then applied at the next midpoint,
$a=0.1485$, and passes as three-branch.  All six access lines complete; each

The second identical-method blind midpoint, $a=0.14825$, also passes as

The branch-conditioned successor passes every support and numerical gate but
rejects its prospectively frozen faster-capture hypothesis.  Three untouched
Sobol ensembles give extra-minus-core restricted mean survival differences of
$+30.50$, $+30.86$, and $+29.98$ time units; their 99% trajectory-bootstrap
intervals are $[27.39,33.55]$, $[27.89,34.01]$, and $[27.25,32.66]$.
The matched half-step result is $+30.90$, only $0.40$ from baseline.  Every
extra-branch trajectory survives 60 residual time units and none survives 180;
the two core domains capture earlier on average but retain a small tail through
270.  The extra branch therefore has delayed but bounded capture, directly
explaining its presence under 300-unit and absence under 360-unit survivor
conditioning.

The first held-out signed-edge test then passes.  At $a=0.148125$, all six PIM
access lines complete and both censor horizons again supply 2097 pairs per

the critical-point count, all 60 lower-support derivative fits are negative:

The finite sampled bracket therefore narrows to $[0.148125,0.14825]$.
