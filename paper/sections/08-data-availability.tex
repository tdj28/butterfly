\section{Code, data, and reproducibility limits}
\label{sec:availability}

The \href{https://github.com/tdj28/butterfly}{public Butterfly repository}
contains the manuscript source, figure generators, environment lockfile,
frozen protocols, and compact experiment receipts. Source revisions and
input hashes identify the recorded computations; a hash is not a substitute
for access to its input file. Most campaign-wide raw orbit and saddle arrays
are not yet distributed, so full reproduction of every main figure is
\emph{not} claimed.

The versioned
\href{https://github.com/tdj28/butterfly/releases/tag/research-core-v1}{research-core-v1 release}
is bound to source commit
\href{https://github.com/tdj28/butterfly/commit/13d1e1aa3a079704b26b372e110614c774b0793d}{\texttt{13d1e1a}}.
It supplies nine data/protocol inputs, their checksums, and the locked
environment. A documented CPU command verifies and replays this subset
from a fresh checkout; the
\href{https://github.com/tdj28/butterfly/blob/main/docs/reproducibility.md}{replay guide}
states its numerical gates and anonymous-download verification.
Its atlas operation redraws cached classifications for one $b=0.2$ panel;
it does not reintegrate the atlas. Its flip and segmented homoclinic checks
reintegrate preserved candidates, not their complete discovery procedures.
The independent collocation pilot is included for inspection, not executed
by that three-example wrapper.

The separate
\href{https://github.com/tdj28/butterfly/releases/tag/research-exp476}{research-exp476 release}
is bound to source commit
\href{https://github.com/tdj28/butterfly/commit/f2fc9cd0885c8343e017a0b46238dff922b89ec7}{\texttt{f2fc9cd}}.
It preserves the full failed-study receipt, saved-mesh diagnostics, scripts
in the tagged source, and checksums. The failed and skipped cases remain
failed and skipped. Replaying a diagnostic is not a successful rerun of the
unfinished accuracy grid.

\href{https://github.com/tdj28/butterfly/blob/research-exp476/paper/supplement/rossler-multib-atlas.gif}{Supplemental Movie S1}
is publicly viewable as an animated GIF. The public core archive does not
contain all eleven source frames needed to regenerate it.
The \href{https://github.com/tdj28/butterfly/tree/main/paper/figures}{figure index}
lists individual generators and their input requirements; several still
need the undistributed historical arrays.
The source-derived symbolic diagram uses tracked transcription inputs and
needs no numerical campaign archive. The algebraic equilibrium check in
Appendix~\ref{supp:equilibria} likewise runs from the source checkout.
The new
\href{https://github.com/tdj28/butterfly/blob/main/docs/experiments/EXP-478-quadratic-symbolic-control.md}{EXP-478 quadratic-control protocol}
links the complete rational root/sign certificates and the separate
word-comparison receipt. Its enumeration and comparison programs were frozen
at public commit
\href{https://github.com/tdj28/butterfly/commit/f4b2ea13c60395713911240b0fbf0ce469850cc4}{\texttt{f4b2ea1}}
before execution. This finite scalar calculation can be rerun with the Python
standard library and requires no private orbit arrays or GPU. The generated
table has a hash-bound provenance file and a byte-for-byte verification mode.

The fresh fold-response result in Figure~\ref{fig:fresh-fold-response} has a
public compact audit receipt and figure generator under EXP-519. A standalone
checker replays its event measurements, contact distances and response
arithmetic using tracked inputs only. The full local audit also replays the
retained trajectory meshes; those raw arrays are not distributed in the
compact receipt. Regenerating this figure therefore does not amount to
independently reintegrating the experiment or repeating its full raw audit.
The periodic-response figure (Figure~\ref{fig:critical-periodic-response})
likewise has a public, hash-bound EXP-521 audit and redraw script retaining
the failed predictor. Its post-run curvature diagnosis is labeled separately.
The subsequent EXP-522 nonlinear refinement also has a complete public compact
audit. The corresponding new trajectory arrays remain local, so the same
raw-data reproducibility limitation applies.

The remaining reproducibility requirement is public dependency closure for
all principal results, including event-transversality diagnostics and
independent-formulation orbit checks. The available subset is a verified
starting point, not a claim that this larger requirement is complete.
