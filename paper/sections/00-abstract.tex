We reassess and extend the proposed topological explanation of periodicity
hubs in the R{\"o}ssler flow using provenance-bearing parameter scans,
periodic-orbit continuation, independent integrators, Floquet analysis, and
return-map reconstructions.  Multi-slice $(a,c)$ atlases at
$b=0.10,\ldots,0.30$ expose a smoothly deforming superstructure of shrimp-like
periodic windows and provide the visual context for the orbit-level tests.
At the reported hub, the small equilibrium is a saddle focus.  Its exact Hopf
locus supplies a verified local endpoint, and the emerging period-1 family is
continued to the reported hub coordinate.  Four exact supercritical flips and
stable children through period 16 are established on this path; the orbit at
the hub nevertheless remains finite-period and far from the equilibrium, so
it is not itself the proposed homoclinic connection.  A separate
stable/unstable-manifold boundary-value calculation does qualify a nearby
homoclinic candidate across DOP853 and Radau, 16 and 32 shooting arcs, and
three shrinking matching radii.  At fixed $c=10.3084$, however, its persistent
value $a\simeq0.182643608174$ differs materially from the printed $a=0.1798$.
Two qualified natural-continuation points point toward the historical fixed-$a$
path, but repeated direct corrections reach reproducible residual floors.
Seventy-two gauge-aligned two-parameter pseudo-arclength points
through as many as 512 arcs pass the matching, arclength, conditioning, and
direction gates.  Together the seventy-five qualified curve points reach within
$1.75\times10^{-5}$ in $a$ of the historical section.  Full-state signed
arclength resolves the first sampled local $a$ minimum and follows the branch
through fifty-eight tangent-recomputed steps toward larger $a$, including
defect-aware reductions and a qualified doubled-step regime, so the pre-fold
projected crossing near
$c=10.31713524$ remains only an extrapolation.  The first residual floor was
not immediate branch termination, although the exact historical intersection
remains unqualified.
On a second path,
identity-safe continuation resolves eight period doublings of a period-5
family through a stable period-1280 child.

On the section used by Barrio, Blesa, and Serrano, the published one- and
two-critical-point return-map controls are reproduced.  Censor-aware
straddle reconstructions of the corresponding nonattracting chaotic saddles
localize a sampled two/three-branch transition at
$a\in[0.1481875,0.14825]$ for $(b,c)=(0.2,20)$.  Recovered unstable periodic
orbits persist across this bracket, while a pre-existing unstable lobe joins
the reconstructed saddle only on the three-branch side, favoring a global
pruning or reinjection mechanism over a local orbit birth or death.

High-resolution scans around two historical period-6 landmarks place them in
distinct stable raster components.  Fifty-eight corrected period-6 flow
orbits show that historical period 6 appears as an eight-phase cycle on the
published section.  None of the sampled representatives satisfies the frozen
double-critical-center test; a denser corrected-orbit mesh localizes a
connected candidate region but fails its preregistered coverage gate.  On its
126 two-step-qualified maps, one signed critical residual crosses zero but the
other remains strictly positive, so neither a direct center nor a simultaneous
bracket is present in the incomplete sampled field.  Quadrupling support below
that field exposes a shallow critical retained by four baseline variants but
removed by one high-smoothing variant.  A nested scale audit nevertheless
qualifies that critical at 94 of 104 points, after which the second residual
remains one-sided over all 1,128 low-smoothing reconstructions.  A lower-$c$
extension finds a bounded 551-orbit stable period-6 strip, and seven
prospectively selected edge brackets all refine to real $-1$ Floquet events.
Exact-Jacobian coupled continuation then passes all 41 frozen points across
$c\in[7.16,7.32]$.  This supplies a dense period-doubling boundary segment for
continued search. Three separately frozen post-flip checks then independently
qualify primitive stable period-12 children paired with unstable period-6
parents. Seven-point continuation toward each event yields square-root opening,
the cubic flip multiplier ratio, cross-solver agreement, and perturbed
attraction at all three slices.  Independent child-family interpolation then
qualifies all 124 points of a 31-by-4 period-12 sheet above the flip curve;
all 31 fixed-$c$ opening fits have exponent $0.50264$--$0.50309$.  This remains
a sampled patch, not a formal global surface or double-critical center.
Pseudo-arclength extends the parent event through $c=8.40309$ and isolates a
historical-section grazing at $c=6.9383180212$: the historical phase count
changes while the exact flow event and Barrio-section count persist. Adaptive
continuation crosses that representation boundary, turns near $c=6.83093$,
and qualifies a second arm through 135 further exact events to
$(a,c)=(0.271267,8.252733)$.  On that returning arm, adaptive continuation
qualifies a stable primitive period-12 strip through 45 exact event samples;
two solvers localize a period-6 flip crossing at
$(a,c)=(0.2406843530,7.6253782976)$ to $3.96\times10^{-9}$ in $c$ and verify
primitive-child versus parent-double-cover behavior bilaterally.  A subsequent
21-point exact-coordinate audit identifies that crossing with the same
returning flip arm to $1.46\times10^{-14}$ in $a$, retracting an apparent
second-boundary inference caused by linear interpolation. At a nearby fixed
returning-arm slice, exact augmented events and independent child switches
qualify six local supercritical doublings through a stable primitive period-768
child. Frozen-representation multiplier agreement proves insufficient for the
next event when unconstrained orbit correction approaches a lower-period double
cover. Coupled 50-digit orbit--antiperiodic-tangent solves with two independent
fourth-order tableaux then converge across three resolutions and qualify a
seventh primitive real-$-1$ event near $a=0.24070100823759$. Repeating the
same coupled, two-tableau protocol on 2,048 segments qualifies an eighth event
near $a=0.24070100822410$, even though it rejects an inherited Float64
micro-bracket. Event-relative solver bracketing and segmented identity then
qualify the period-3072 birth as locally subcritical. Same-map
4,096/8,192-step switches recover an immediate stable period-1536 daughter
with quadratic lower-$a$ opening and qualify the seventh birth as locally
supercritical. A nominal higher-$a$ Float64 daughter initially produces an
apparent stable/stable pair, but 50-digit Armijo correction closes it below
$1.20\times10^{-23}$ and collapses its primitive amplitude below
$7.4\times10^{-20}$ in the 4,096-step map. An independent 8,192-step replay
from the unchanged seed reaches $7.22\times10^{-30}$ matching and
$6.03\times10^{-25}$ amplitude, reproducing the doubled-parent collapse.
Fresh same-map continuation then connects the exact immediate daughter to the
eighth event; shared-phase node RMS is $6.35\times10^{-18}$ at the event.
Thus the computations substantially strengthen the
finite cascade and return-map parts of the Jones picture while keeping the
homoclinic endpoint, the global double-critical-center claim, a continuous
topology-transition curve, and a complete explanation of the full parameter
plane open.
