Why do neighboring periodic windows in the R{\"o}ssler flow form shrimp chains
and nested spirals? Jones's symbolic explanation makes a specific prediction:
changing reinjection inserts an inner return into an orbit's itinerary,
linking period $p$ to period $p+1$. This is different from period doubling.
We make that proposed connection the central test of a computational
reassessment of the Jones and Barrio--Blesa--Serrano studies.

The source-derived diagram retains all 23 reported words through period seven
and distinguishes the evidence behind their links. Independent reconstructions
qualify a local operational alphabet, but not a generating partition or the
reported critical itineraries. A test at one landmark's printed coordinates
fails both the required cross-coordinate partition agreement and the
period-six word comparison. It is a failed local reconstruction, not a
qualified counterexample to symbolic ordering. The source supplies an
approximate landmark, not an identified exact word center.
A separate exact quadratic-map control recovers the unimodal subset and
its order under a predeclared notation convention. The third-branch words
and all flow connections are outside that test.

Corrected periodic orbits, independent integrators, and chaotic-saddle
reconstructions qualify several local ingredients of the explanation. These
include a finite-horizon two-to-three-branch scalar-return classifier change,
with a pre-existing unstable lobe sampled only on its tested three-branch
side, connected periodic-orbit structure, and a nearby
homoclinic candidate checked with a second boundary formulation. These results
come from separate parameter slices: they have not yet been linked into a
window-to-window symbolic transition. The printed homoclinic coordinate and
the candidate's organizing role remain open.

The decisive missing test is therefore the connection between measured
reinjection and the predicted extra symbol. The present results provide
an auditable basis for that test, not a completed R{\"o}ssler symbolic enumeration or
an explanation of the whole parameter plane.
