\section{Topology and symbolic tests still required}

The original Jones mechanism proposes that, while a stable orbit is followed
around a nested parameter-space spiral, the dynamically relevant return
relation alternates between two and three branches and the third branch changes
its reinjection geometry \citep{jones2012topological}.  The Barrio analysis
provides a more explicit TBA and double-superstability construction
\citep{barrio2012topological}.  The modern experiments have not yet established
that global curve or the reinjection mechanism.

The first required test constructs a branch-count oracle on a frozen section
and invariant domain.  It must locate critical points with numerical
uncertainty, reject extrema created only by projection or poor sampling,
continue the two/three-branch boundary, and repeat under declared perturbations
of the section.  A single orbit's change in historical crossing count is
insufficient, particularly in light of the section grazing found above.

The second test recovers the original parameter paths $L_1$ and $L_2$ and
generates orbit permutations, kneading data, critical itineraries, and Floquet
stability through the eighth resolved doubling.  It then searches prospectively for the first
high-period mismatch, as required by the two-dimensional limitation emphasized
by Holmes \citep{holmes1984bifurcation}.  Agreement over a finite range will be
reported as finite correspondence, not conjugacy; the prior experimental
finite-order result of Lefranc et al. remains part of the novelty boundary
\citep{lefranc1994combining}.

The third test defines reinjection as an observable of the return map rather
than as the position of an abstract branched manifold in phase space.  It will
compare coordinates, branch order, critical values, winding/linking data, and
candidate template invariants.  Its decisive test is prospective: does the
observable predict held-out $p\mathbin{\to}p+1$ connections beyond predictions
already supplied by TBA and homoclinic geometry?

Finally, the original unfolded-spiral figure will be replaced by a generated,
receipt-bound figure.  Parameter arclength and angle, branch count, critical
values, periodic-orbit points, symbolic words, and both successful and
ambiguous transitions must be visible together.  Until those tests pass, the
topological mechanism remains a structured hypothesis.
