\section{Symbolic dynamics: how the proposed chains connect shrimp}
\label{sec:mechanism}

The central hypothesis links three observations: the path of a shrimp chain
in parameter space, the geometry of reinjection, and the order of visits
within a periodic orbit. Following a connected spiral is proposed to insert
an additional inner return, increasing its period by one. The symbolic word
records where that return appears. Figure~\ref{fig:symbolic-chain} shows
the finite chain from the original Figure~6
\citep{jones2012topological}. It is a redraw of the reported construction;
its arrows are not new numerical results from the present study.

\subsection{Reading an orbit as a word}

Choose a return section and a scalar representation of its return map.
Record the branch visited at each return. In Jones's notation, the digits
$0,1,2$ name branches, $C$ marks the critical point retained from the
unimodal regime, and $D$ marks the additional critical point in the bimodal
regime. The source associates $2$ with the third-branch excursion and $0$
with the innermost deposited return. A word such as $CD001$ has five
symbols and describes a period-five critical itinerary in that
representation, not a flight time of five. A word containing both $C$ and
$D$ is intended to identify a doubly critical itinerary at a shrimp center.

The word is cyclic: choosing another starting point rotates the same
sequence. Reversing the sequence is a different operation and is not
silently identified with a rotation. These labels describe a specified
scalar return representation; they are not automatically invariants under
changing the section or projecting the full flow differently. Assigning a
critical symbol to numerical data therefore needs a measured critical
location and an explicit uncertainty criterion.

Three uses of symbolic dynamics must be kept distinct. An \emph{encoding}
assigns labels to observed returns. A \emph{finite ordering comparison}
tests specified words, cyclic permutations, and their relative order against
a one-dimensional model. A \emph{generating partition} or a
\emph{topological template} additionally needs a justified construction on
the invariant set; neither follows from successful word matching alone.
Earlier R{\"o}ssler UPO/template work \citep{letellier1995unstable} and
rigorous symbolic window searches \citep{galias2025symbolic} are distinct
benchmarks, not new methods introduced by the present reassessment.

\subsection{Why the proposed chain gains one symbol}

Jones's geometric picture is that, as a stable window is followed around
the parameter-space spiral, the third-branch reinjection moves around the
central equilibrium. On returning to the two-branch side, the excursion
leaves an additional inner visit. The original paper illustrates this with
\begin{equation}
  C100\ \longrightarrow\ C200\ \longrightarrow\ C1000.
  \label{eq:jones-insertion-example}
\end{equation}
The first change uses the third branch; the second replaces that excursion
with an extra inner return. This is a proposed period-four to period-five
connection, not a period doubling.

At the reported doubly critical itineraries, the finite arrows have the
compact form
\begin{equation}
  CDw\ \longrightarrow\ CD0w,
  \label{eq:jones-zero-insertion}
\end{equation}
where $w$ is the unchanged suffix. For example,
$CD01\to CD001\to CD0001\to CD00001$ connects the reported periods
four through seven. Ten arrows in the source are reported as matched by
both state-space geometry and symbols. The eleventh,
$CD0111\to CD00111$, has only the source's visual-attractor evidence.
The lower-period links from $C2$ and $C21$, and the separate doubling
$C1\to CD01$, have distinct styles in the redraw.

The isolated-shrimp origins are also part of the explanation: the source
proposes that some sequences start new spirals instead of continuing a
preceding one. Their marked nodes are retained, along with words for which
no connecting edge was transcribed. A missing edge is left missing; the
insertion rule is not used to invent a complete network or extrapolate
the finite construction to all periods.

\subsection{Turning the chain into a tested explanation}

The decisive symbolic test must locate centers using orbit and critical-point
measurements without consulting the desired word, then encode the corrected
orbits and follow each proposed connection. The same experiment should
retain missing words, failed links, and the first disagreement. A long
period-doubling cascade cannot substitute for that comparison.
Related finite ordering was already observed in experimental flows
\citep{lefranc1994combining}, and two-dimensional return maps can exhibit
high-period orderings unavailable to one-dimensional unimodal maps
\citep{holmes1984bifurcation}.
The finite-pattern literature and topological-template framework provide
distinct reference objects \citep{metropolis1973finite,gilmore1998topological};
neither can be inferred from a visually similar scalar plot.

The geometric test is equally important: measure how stable and unstable
manifolds meet, or which symbolic trajectories become accessible, and ask
whether that predicts the observed extra return at held-out parameters.
The measurement must survive changes of section and numerical resolution.
A finite saddle sample that misses a lobe cannot by itself prove the lobe
absent from the infinite-time invariant set.

For mechanistic closure we require more than an unsigned nearest-neighbor
distance. A prospectively specified stable/unstable-manifold or pruning
construction must provide a signed separation or access observable on a
common transverse section. Its sign change must survive refinement and
admissible section changes, and predict a held-out window connection or
symbolic insertion. Identifying the relevant invariant manifolds is part of
the test; it cannot be assumed from the lobe association. This criterion is
an open research requirement, not a calculation completed by this revision.

The intended next figure is a \emph{computed} unfolded spiral: parameter
position, return geometry, corrected orbit points, and words shown together.
It would test the source-derived chain displayed here, not replace the need
to show it. Section~\ref{sec:symbolic-results} reports how far the present

and unsuccessful searches. Establishing the reinjection-to-word connection
would answer the original explanatory question more directly than another
doubling or another short continuation step.
