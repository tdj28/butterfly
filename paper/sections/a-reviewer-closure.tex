\section{Reviewer concerns and remaining closure tests}
\label{supp:reviewers}

The original reviews identified explanatory gaps as well as presentation
problems. Better orbit numerics address some of those gaps, but a deeper
cascade does not automatically answer a question about symbolic ordering or
reinjection. The table below separates work completed from the test still
needed. Experiment details are in Appendix~\ref{supp:experiments}; the
repository claim ledger gives the full acceptance criteria.

The high-period results are valuable evidence about orbit computation.
They do not replace the RVR-003--006 mechanism tests. The incorrect
full-flow zero-multiplier rationale and the false period-1536 daughter are
retained as explicit corrections in the experimental record.
