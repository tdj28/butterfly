\section{Conclusion}

The computations support several parts of the Jones picture: the local
saddle-focus equilibrium, a Hopf-born periodic family with successive
doublings, and robust two- and three-branch scalar return relations on
specified sections. They also expose limits that change how those results
should be stated. A scalar projection is not automatically a
one-dimensional dynamical model, a section-crossing change need not be a
flow bifurcation, and a corrected high-period candidate can still be a
multiple cover of a lower-period orbit.

The homoclinic calculation is encouraging but incomplete. Independent
integrators, shooting meshes, and matching radii support a candidate near
$a=0.182643608174$ at $(b,c)=(0.2,10.3084)$. Its difference from the printed
$a=0.1798$ exceeds rounding at the published precision. Locating this candidate
does not exclude a different connection at the printed point or establish
that it organizes the observed hub. The eighty continuation points shown in
Figure~\ref{fig:homoclinic-continuation} are the EXP-472 snapshot; EXP-473 adds
an eighty-first accepted point to the repository record. The sampled
outgoing branch remains above the fixed-$a$ slice, but the small singular
values require a parameter-error audit before its apparent turn is assigned
a precise physical location. Neither uniqueness nor global nonintersection
has been established.

The most direct progress toward the proposed topology mechanism is the
saddle-defined classification interval at $(b,c)=(0.2,20)$. The eleven
recovered unstable periodic-orbit families persist across it, while a
pre-existing unstable lobe is represented only in the three-branch saddle
samples. An independently frozen midpoint reproduces that association.
This supports testing pruning or reinjection through manifold geometry; it
does not establish an exact manifold contact or a continuous transition curve.

The finite cascades and period-6 flip arms provide useful orbit structure.
Seven supercritical births on the returning family are numerically qualified,
with evidence for a subcritical eighth birth. The apparent higher-$a$
period-1536 daughter is retracted after higher-precision correction collapses
it to the doubled parent. The failed symbolic-center searches and numerical
controls remain part of the record. None of these results establishes
global logistic conjugacy, universality, or exact full-flow superstability.

The next decisive calculations are therefore a convergent manifold-contact
residual, an independently reproduced homoclinic boundary-value solution with
parameter uncertainty, and a complete finite symbolic comparison through the
original period-seven claim. Those tests should produce an unfolded-spiral
figure whose orbit connections and uncertainties can be read directly from
the data. Replication on two structurally different flows should follow before
extending the approach to dozens of systems. The present result is a stronger,
more testable local account of periodicity hubs, with the global explanation
still open.
