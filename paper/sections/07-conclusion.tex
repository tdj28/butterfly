\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The central explanatory claim is that changing reinjection produces a
specific extra symbol, connecting neighboring periodic windows into chains
and spirals. Jones's finite construction makes that claim explicit and
testable. This reassessment qualifies a local operational dictionary and
several orbit and return-map ingredients, but has not yet reproduced the
reinjection-to-word transition.

The distinction sets the meaning of the other results. Connected periodic
families establish local structure; saddle-lobe observations suggest what
to measure in reinjection; and independent formulations support a nearby
homoclinic candidate. These are contributions toward the explanation, not
substitutes for testing the symbolic links. The printed homoclinic point,
global transition curve, and whole-plane organization remain unresolved.
Corrections to the modern reconstruction remain in the technical record.

The decisive next advance is to locate a center without fitting the target
word and test one predicted $p\to p+1$ connection at held-out parameters.
An unfolded-spiral figure should show the parameter path, corrected orbits,
return geometry, and words together, including any disagreement. That would
test why neighboring shrimp connect, rather than merely add another
well-resolved example of a shrimp or a doubling cascade.
