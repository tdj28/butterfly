# The manuscript now carries the visual argument

Status: rebuilt, compiled, and visually inspected

The working manuscript had accumulated a detailed experiment narrative but no
figure environments. Its abstract had also grown to roughly three pages. That
was not an adequate presentation of a project whose central object is the
geometry of shrimp-like periodic windows and their organization across a
parameter plane.

## What changed

The abstract is now a 304-word summary, and nine figures are integrated into
the argument:

1. eleven fixed-b recurrence atlases from b=0.10 through b=0.30;
2. a global b=0.2 atlas paired with the 50-times-refined period-6 landmark
   band;
3. the exact small-equilibrium Hopf locus;
4. the continued period-1 family from Hopf toward the reported hub;
5. the corrected period-5 flip/fold continuation;
6. that invariant curve overlaid on its coarse shrimp-scale raster context;
7. independent normal-form opening diagnostics;
8. the published two- and three-branch Barrio-section return-map controls; and
9. the dense 2,511-point period-6 corrected-orbit mesh.

Supplemental Movie S1 animates the eleven parameter-plane slices across b.
Publication copies, source experiment paths, hashes, regeneration commands,
and generated-figure receipts are recorded under paper/figures and
paper/supplement.

## Scientific boundaries preserved

The new presentation deliberately distinguishes three evidence levels.
Finite-time rasters reveal the shrimp superstructure and guide searches.
Corrected periodic-orbit curves and Floquet calculations establish invariant
local bifurcations. Return-map reconstructions test the section-dependent
two/three-branch mechanism. Captions state these boundaries explicitly.

The figures do not promote a raster component to a continued orbit family.
They also do not turn the failed EXP-198 coverage gate into a center claim.
Figure 9 shows the boundary-touching 152-point center component and states
that only 685 points qualify against the frozen 1,000-point minimum.
