# EXP-242 exposes an eigenvalue swap; EXP-243 is frozen

The 21 exact period-24 spectra behave smoothly, but EXP-242's nearest-neighbor
identity rule jumps onto the collapsed transverse multiplier after index 1 and
therefore misses the visible `+0.450252` to `-1.066595` crossing.

EXP-243 reclassifies the immutable spectra only when the two transverse
moduli are separated by at least `1e8` at every row. It requires exactly one
real-`-1` bracket and changes neither integration nor continuation data.
