# EXP-190 rejects one neighborhood and exposes a better lead

EXP-190 completed on a secure RTX A4500 from clean commit `7eefb28`. The full
receipt matched remotely and locally, and all workers were terminated. Every
one of the 65 candidates is robustly two-branch at both RK4 steps; no candidate
has the two critical intervals required by the frozen center locator.

The failure is local and representation-bounded. It rules out the first
landmark's Floquet-zero neighborhood, not the co-discovered double-
superstability mechanism. An explicitly labeled post-result diagnostic then
tested Jones's other exact period-6 landmark `(a,c)=(0.215,7.6)` on the
independently published Barrio section. Its z return relation resolves three
branches under all five variants from 17,145 survivor pairs; the y relation is
three-branch in four variants and loses the shallow extremum only at the
strictest prominence. One critical is already close to an orbit point while
the other remains far away, making this a topology-qualified search
neighborhood rather than an exact center claim.

The next prospective experiment maps the complete local period-6 window
around this second landmark at high resolution. It will then restrict direct
two-critical optimization to corrected stable period-6 pixels without using a
Figure 6 word.
