# EXP-228 rejects the broad distinct-curve claim

EXP-228 accepts 53 exact real-`-1` events and both independent Radau terminal
controls, but both directions stop when the interpolation-based distance from
EXP-217 falls below the frozen `5e-8` magnitude. The correctors themselves
remain regular and every invariant gate passes.

Same-coordinate diagnostics show that EXP-227 and freshly corrected EXP-217
events agree near machine precision. The former `5.6e-7--5.8e-7` separation
was the error of linearly interpolating a curved event arm. EXP-229 is frozen
to confirm this at every EXP-227 coordinate and prevent the false distinctness
inference from propagating into the paper.
