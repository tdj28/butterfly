# DEC-001 - Independent co-discovery wording

Date: 2026-08-06
Status: accepted working characterization

## Decision

The project will describe the overlapping 2012 results of Timothy D. Jones and
Roberto Barrio, Fernando Blesa, and Sergio Serrano as an **independent,
contemporaneous co-discovery**.

The shared 2012 result in scope is the explicit connection between a transition
in unimodal/two-branch versus bimodal/three-branch return-map structure and
periodicity-hub/shrimp organization, including its relationship to doubly-
superstable shrimp centers. This decision does not collapse the papers'
distinct methods or claims into one contribution.

## Prior-work qualification

The working literature review reports that a 2011 Barrio-Blesa-Serrano-
Shilnikov paper had already connected hub focal points with Shilnikov
homoclinics and identified a boundary between spiral/unimodal and screw-like/
bimodal attractors. That primary source has not yet been added to or verified
within this repository.

Accordingly, the project does **not** describe the 2012 papers as independently
discovering every ingredient. It describes them as near-simultaneous, largely
independent extensions of a common foundation, co-discovering the boundary's
explicit role in shrimp and periodicity-hub organization.

## Documented chronology

| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Barrio-Blesa-Serrano manuscript received by *Physical Review Letters* | 2011-12-16 |
| Jones arXiv v1 publicly posted | 2012-01-20 |
| Barrio-Blesa-Serrano revised manuscript received | 2012-03-23 |
| Barrio-Blesa-Serrano published in *Physical Review Letters* | 2012-05-25 |

The Barrio-Blesa-Serrano submission therefore predates the Jones arXiv posting,
while the Jones public disclosure predates the Barrio-Blesa-Serrano journal
publication. No evidence of derivation in either direction is present in the
current project record. An earlier public Barrio-Blesa-Serrano preprint has not
yet been identified by this project.

## Required wording

Preferred:

> Building on a common 2011 foundation, Jones and Barrio, Blesa, and Serrano
> independently and nearly simultaneously connected the topological return-map
> transition to Rössler periodicity-hub and shrimp organization. Jones's arXiv
> disclosure preceded publication of the Barrio-Blesa-Serrano article, although
> their manuscript had already been received by the journal.

Avoid:

- claiming that either paper derived the result from the other without new
  documentary evidence;
- using journal publication date alone to assign priority;
- saying that the entire papers are duplicate discoveries; or
- implying that the 2012 papers first discovered every homoclinic or
  spiral-to-screw ingredient; or
- extending co-discovery wording to Jones's mutant-shrimp or rotating-
  reinjection hypotheses without demonstrating corresponding overlap.

## Distinct extensions

The papers are complementary rather than redundant:

- **Barrio-Blesa-Serrano:** defines the TBA using chaotic invariant sets across
  both chaotic and regular regions; computes chaotic saddles; relates TBA to
  superstability-curve tangency; partitions a representative shrimp; and gives
  experimental circuit evidence.
- **Jones:** proposes Hopf-to-homoclinic slices with logistic-like window
  ordering; follows inter-period symbolic progression; identifies mutant
  shrimps; and proposes rotating third-branch reinjection as a generative
  mechanism for `p -> p+1` spirals.
