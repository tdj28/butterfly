# FND-036 — Phase-resolved capture rejects the atlas contrast

Status: qualified negative result from prospective EXP-144

## Finding

All 408 exact-return trajectories and all 24 phase-transported Floquet seeds
complete successfully. Transport validation is decisive: maximum signed-
multiplier relative error is `0.001875`, and maximum transverse residual ratio
is `0.002057`, against frozen limits of `0.01`.

The proposed capture observable fails. Only 13 of 24 phase/endpoint summaries
meet the nested 17/9-amplitude-grid tolerance. None of the four EXP-143
candidates retains a five-return or larger endpoint difference at all three
orbit phases. Lower family 03 lag 7 and lower family 02 lag 5 each reverse the
endpoint direction between the 64- and 96-return summaries at one phase.

Thus the capture-fraction contrast that helped rank EXP-143's shortlist is not
a phase-, density-, and horizon-robust signature of the branch opening.

## Consequence for Jones

This does **not** falsify Jones's observed two/three-branch change or his
reinjection hypothesis. The 2012 paper did not claim that finite-horizon
capture time was the invariant responsible for the topology change. It does
reject one modern attempt to turn the qualitative reinjection picture into a
predictive scalar observable.

The positive UPO evidence remains unchanged: all eleven recovered primitive
families persist across the local bracket, and their manifold seeds validate.
The next mechanism test must therefore compare geometry or symbolic
connectivity directly—such as an unstable/stable-manifold intersection or a
pruning residual—rather than using capture timing as its proxy.
