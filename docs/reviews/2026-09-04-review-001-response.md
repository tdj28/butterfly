# Review 001: assessment and manuscript response

The supplied review concerns the earlier manuscript, before the symbolic
explanation was moved to the front. It is treated as an external assessment,
not as an instruction to assert its conclusions or execute its suggested
experiments. This response paraphrases the scientific issues; the private
review and its embedded citation tokens are not copied into the repository.

## Overall judgment

The central criticism is right: local numerical successes have not established
the proposed invariant reinjection-to-symbolic mechanism. The recent
mechanism-first organization is retained. The paper is a computational
reassessment directed toward that explanation, not a claim to have completed
it. Symbolic centrality and honest evidence limits are compatible.

Several additional objections were still valid in the current draft: absent
PDF archive links, an incomplete literature comparison, ambiguous numerical
"exact" language, insufficiently explicit quotient definitions, within-net
bootstrap inference, and too little discussion of the second equilibrium.

## Specific wording and presentation changes

- The fixed-a orbit path is an endpoint-matched L2-like **surrogate**, not
  recovered historical path equations or a demonstrated homoclinic endpoint.
- The eleven recovered UPOs are a nonexhaustive library. An unobserved orbit
  bifurcation is not excluded by their persistence.
- The scalar-superstability clarification corrects our retired Floquet-zero
  proxy, not the validity of scalar reduced-map superstability itself.
- Jones's reported b=0.2–2.0 range is acknowledged. The local eleven-slice
  atlas does not validate that wider range.
- Main return-map captions now state the cubic fit, bins, smoothing budget,
  occupied support, and sensitivity envelopes without inventing statistical
  confidence bounds.
- Added a compact four-row PIM endpoint/horizon table and a source-linked CSV.
  This is a selected summary, not every historical classifier point.
- Corrected numerical "exact" language in the prose. Frozen figure assets,
  identifiers, and receipts remain unchanged; any legacy figure label is
  explicitly described as a numerical/discretized-map label, not a proof.

Remaining presentation work includes larger print-scale versions of dense

## Places where the review needs qualification

1. The earlier abstract/framing criticism was already partly addressed by the
   mechanism-first revision. The new edits preserve, rather than demote, the
   symbolic explanation.
2. The review's Bonatto–Gallas 2008 experimental attribution is too strong.
   The checked primary description concerns a numerical circuit model,
   distinguished from later experiments. The paper uses the narrower claim.
3. Vitolo et al.'s folded homoclinic structures are not the same mathematical
   object as our folded flip curve, and their z equation differs from this
   repository's standard convention. Matching parameter names is insufficient.
4. The review's proposed thresholds, ≥20-scramble example, and amplitude
   counts are suggested designs, not universal mathematical requirements.
   Any successor must freeze justified criteria before target execution.
5. A positive Shilnikov spectral quantity uses **reversed time** for the
   small equilibrium. The forward signed sum is negative. The condition is
   local spectral context, not evidence that a loop exists.

## Follow-up: independent symbolic verification request

The subsequent audit caught two remaining overstatements. The abstract and
main results now say that EXP-186 failed **partition parity as well as word
recovery**; it was not a qualified counterexample to symbolic ordering.
The distant EXP-185 alphabet control is explicitly separated from the tested
landmark. Supplement S3 no longer says the negative center search rules out
a sampling-power failure: unresolved/subthreshold critical points remain
possible under the finite-horizon protocol, including at sampled parameters.

New computation is tracked separately in
[EXP-477](../experiments/EXP-477-symbolic-center-pilot.md) and
[EXP-478](../experiments/EXP-478-quadratic-symbolic-control.md). The former is
a word-independent flow-center scout; the latter is a finite scalar-map
control. Neither a source redraw nor a successful scalar comparison is to be
reported as independent verification of the Rössler chains. Their results
must be assessed on their own receipts, not inferred from this review response.
