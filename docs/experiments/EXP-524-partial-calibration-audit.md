# EXP-524 — audit the complete calibration retained by EXP-523

Status: outcome-informed recovery design, not a completed scientific result.
The [recovery cycle](../../experiments/manifests/EXP-524-recovery-cycle.json)
records the explicit 2026-09-11 human approval, C524/E524/F524 lineage and
single attempt. No paid review was requested, under the human-directed policy.

## Question and claim boundary

Do all eight complete calibration points retained before EXP-523's timeout
reproduce under its frozen midpoint/fold/guard/cycle replay, complete polynomial
root census, physical-root correspondence and separately coded scalar response
checks? A passing audit can establish usable historical calibration data. It
cannot establish a continuation step, C/D dictionary, grazing endpoint,
homoclinic connection, Jones insertion arrow or entire-plane explanation.

The audit's integrity pass and the points' scientific qualification are
different. Every complete point is replayed even if its geometry fails. Report
all eight points and their combined response. Any integrity mismatch fails the
audit; unqualified geometry or response blocks the proposed successor.

## Numerical equivalence and independence limits

Reuse byte-identical EXP-523 point replay components: all fold constructions,
DOP853 and Radau, both cycle phases, exact polynomial census and root identity,
historical/Barrio 6/8 counts, and all sixteen fold/eight gap variants. Existing
separately coded scalar checks reconstruct derivatives and curvature. No new
predictor is computed in this audit; that belongs in a separately frozen
successor. Shared geometry means this is not independent-team replication.
No new IVP is allowed: a tested solver/alias deny guard surrounds replay.

The twelve-hour timer covers preservation checks, all raw replay and output
serialization. Read-only historical input is never modified. The new attempt
has an exclusive marker and output directory; exceptions preserve a failure
receipt and partial progress. The output cap is 128 MiB, with a 1 MiB failure
reserve and an 8 GiB free-space floor. The new initial reserve is calculated
for this read-only audit, not a relaxation of EXP-523's collection limits.
